Simple workflow

Connect your sheet once, open an eBay order, review the extracted details, and add the order.

Designed for organised records

The extension can capture order number, product title, order date, buyer details, quantity, colour, size, and order earnings from supported eBay pages.

Direct to your Sheet

Your connected Google Sheet is selected by you. The extension uses the first worksheet automatically.

Clickable order records

When an eBay order URL is available, the order number can be stored as a clickable link back to the eBay order.

Helpful error messages

When a supported action fails, the extension shows a clear message so you know what to check next.
